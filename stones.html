Stone Education

Look beyond
the colour.

Choosing a stone is about more than appearance. Colour, brilliance, transparency, character and setting all influence how a gemstone becomes part of a piece of jewellery.

Colour is one of the first things we notice in a gemstone. Tone and saturation can create everything from delicate softness to dramatic depth.

Natural stones can contain internal characteristics. These details are part of what makes each gemstone visually individual.

A well-considered cut can influence how light travels through a stone, affecting its brilliance, sparkle and overall visual presence.

The ideal stone should complement the jewellery design, intended wear and personal aesthetic. The Jewelica can help you explore the possibilities.
