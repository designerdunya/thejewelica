Gold Jewellery

Gold Details
Gold jewellery can be created in different purity levels. The appropriate option depends on the intended design, durability preferences and jewellery type. During a consultation, we can discuss the available purity choices for your selected piece.
Yellow gold offers a warm traditional character, while other gold tones can create a more contemporary appearance. Your choice can be guided by personal preference, existing jewellery and the overall look you want to achieve.
Many gold jewellery designs are suitable for regular wear, but the ideal choice depends on the design and construction. Removing jewellery during activities that may expose it to harsh chemicals, impact or excessive friction is recommended.

Diamond Jewellery

Diamond Guidance
Consider the diamond's cut, colour, clarity and carat weight, along with the overall appearance and the jewellery design in which it will be set. A balanced combination of these characteristics helps create a piece suited to your preferences.
Keep diamond jewellery away from harsh chemicals and store individual pieces separately to reduce scratching. Gentle cleaning and periodic inspection can also help maintain the appearance and security of the setting.
Periodic inspection can help identify signs of wear around settings, clasps and other components before a small issue becomes more serious. THE JEWELICA Care Studio can assist with jewellery inspection and restoration needs.

Yes. Gemstone preference can be part of a bespoke jewellery consultation. The stone, jewellery type, setting and overall design can be discussed together to create a cohesive concept.

Jewellery Care

Care Studio
Cleaning requirements vary according to the jewellery, metal and gemstones involved. Avoid harsh household chemicals and abrasive materials. For valuable or delicate pieces, professional cleaning and inspection is a safer approach.
Store pieces separately in a clean, dry environment. Keeping individual items apart can reduce scratching, tangling and unnecessary contact between different jewellery surfaces.
It is generally wise to remove jewellery before swimming, exercising, bathing or using household chemicals. Perfume, lotions and other products can also affect certain jewellery surfaces and gemstones.
