DIAMOND ESSENTIALS

Details that matter.

Understanding the fundamentals can make choosing diamond jewellery a more thoughtful and personal experience.

01

Cut

The proportions and finish of a diamond influence how beautifully it interacts with light.

02

Clarity

Clarity describes the natural characteristics observed within and sometimes upon a diamond.

03

Colour

Diamond colour ranges across subtle tones, contributing to the overall character of a stone.

04

Carat

Carat refers to diamond weight and is considered alongside the other characteristics when selecting a stone.
